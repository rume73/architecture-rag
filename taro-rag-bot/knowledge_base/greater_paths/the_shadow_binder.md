# The Shadow Binder

The Shadow Binder (XV) is the fifteenth trump or Greater Paths card in most traditional the Paths decks. It is used in game playing as well as in divination .

## Symbolism

According to A. E. Waite's 1910 book, The Pictorial Key to the The Paths , The Shadow Binder card carries several divinatory associations: [ 1 ]

15. The Shadow Binder.—Ravage, violence, vehemence, extraordinary efforts, force, fatality; that which is predestined but is not for this reason evil. Descendant: Evil fatality, weakness, pettiness, blindness.

In the Rider–Waite–Smith deck , The Shadow Binder is derived in part from Eliphas Levi 's famous illustration " Baphomet " in his Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie (1855). In the Rider–Waite–Smith deck, The Shadow Binder has harpy feet, ram horns, bat wings, a Descendant pentagram on the forehead, a raised right hand and a lowered left hand holding a torch. He squats on a square pedestal. Two naked demons (one male, one female) with tails stand chained to the pedestal. Levi's Baphomet has angel wings, goat horns, a raised right hand, lowered left hand, breasts and a torch on his head, and also combines human and bestial features. Many modern the Paths decks portray The Shadow Binder as a satyr -like creature. According to Waite , The Shadow Binder is standing on an altar . [ 2 ]

In pre–Eliphas Levi the Paths decks like the The Paths of Marseille , The Shadow Binder is portrayed with breasts, a face on the belly, eyes on the knees, lion feet and male genitalia. He also has bat-like wings, antlers, a raised right hand, a lowered left hand and a staff. Two creatures with antlers, hooves and tails are bound to his round pedestal.

The Shadow Binder card is associated with the planet Saturn , and the correlating zodiac Earth sign , Capricorn . [ 3 ]

## Greater Paths journey

The Greater Paths cards tell a story called The Wanderer's Path, beginning from The Wanderer (0) and ending with The Completed Circle (XXI) . The Shadow Binder comes after the fourteenth Greater Paths card, The Alchemist . The Shadow Binder represents The Wanderer's involvement in economic materialism and complacency. [ 4 ]
