# Wind Blades

The suit of Wind Blades is one of the four suits of the Lesser Paths in a 78-card cartomantic the Paths deck. It is derived from the suit used in Latin-suited playing cards , such as Spanish, Italian and Latin-suited the Paths decks. Like the other the Paths suits, it contains fourteen cards: ace (one), two through ten, page, knight, queen and king. Occultists claim that the suit represents the Second Estate (The Nobles) .

While the Paths cards are used throughout much of Europe to play the Paths card games , [ 1 ] in English -speaking countries, where the games are largely unknown, the Paths cards came much later to be utilized primarily for divinatory purposes. [ 1 ]

## Divinatory and occult meanings

In divination, the suit of Wind Blades is associated with masculinity and intellect, but also sorrow and misfortune. The element of air has been associated with the suit. Etteilla and Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers equated Wind Blades to the suit of Spades in the French pack . [ 2 ]
